**Allocard — company spending without prefunding employee wallets.**

Allocard is a corporate spending platform for small crypto-native teams. It lets companies give employees permission to spend directly from a shared treasury, with clear budgets and restrictions. Unspent funds remain together and under company control.

**Who it’s for**

Small teams that regularly make crypto payments and want employees to pay vendors independently, without repeatedly involving the founder or finance manager.

**The problem**

Sending money to employee wallets fragments the company’s capital and creates balances that need tracking and recovery. Keeping every payment with the treasury manager creates approval delays and administrative work.

Allocard gives employees spending access while keeping unused funds in the company treasury.

**How it works**

1. **Create a company workspace.** Sign up using email and set up the treasury.
2. **Invite employees.** Add teammates by email.
3. **Describe their spending access.** Use ordinary language to specify budgets, approved recipients, expiry dates and approval requirements.
4. **Review and confirm.** Allocard translates the request into explicit, supported rules for the manager to approve.
5. **Let employees pay.** Employees make payments within their permissions, using company funds.
6. **Manage access.** Managers track spending and can change or revoke future spending permissions.

For example:

> “Let Ada spend up to 500 USDC this month with these approved vendors. Require approval for payments above 100 USDC.”

**Core product capabilities**

| Capability | Customer benefit |
|---|---|
| Shared company treasury | Avoid distributing unused balances across employee wallets |
| Employee spending permissions | Let teammates operate independently within limits |
| Plain-language policy setup | Reduce configuration work and unfamiliar terminology |
| Contract-enforced restrictions | Apply supported rules even when someone bypasses the interface |
| Email-based onboarding | Make joining familiar and straightforward |
| Spending records and access management | Help managers understand payments and control future access |

**What makes Allocard different**

Allocard’s intended advantage is the complete spending experience: fast setup, understandable controls and smooth employee payments. Delegation is the underlying mechanism; everyday usability is the product.

Its success should be measured by how quickly teams can get started, whether employees can complete payments without assistance, and how much coordination the platform removes.

**Product boundaries**

Allocard enables supported crypto payments. It does not initially provide bank-issued corporate cards or universal merchant acceptance.

Employee allowances are permissions, not ownership of funds or necessarily reserved balances. Payments depend on available treasury funds. Revoking access prevents future spending; it does not reverse settled payments.
